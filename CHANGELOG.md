# Changelog

Speed Dial updates itself. When a new version arrives, Harbor shows a small "Speed Dial updated" card with what changed.
You can also see your version and this list any time: open **Speed & sleep** and click **What's new** at the bottom of Custom speed.

## v1.4.0 (2026-11-21)

- Faster updates: new versions arrive within a few minutes, or seconds after restarting Harbor
- If Custom speed can't find Harbor's Speed & sleep menu, it tries a second way and tells you instead of failing silently

## v1.3.1 (2026-11-21)

- Fixed: Custom speed (dial, Remember for this show, Use for all videos, Saved shows) didn't appear in Harbor's
  Speed & sleep menu, because Harbor wraps that button in a tooltip

## v1.3.0 (2026-11-21)

- Saved shows: see every show's speed and remove any of them
- Update notice when a new version arrives, plus the version and What's new in the menu

## v1.2.0 (2026-11-21)

- Lives inside Harbor's Speed & sleep menu, no extra buttons on screen
- Remember a speed per show; it beats "Use for all videos"
- Fixed: the next episode sometimes didn't get its saved speed

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-21)

- First release: dial, any speed from 0.1× to 8×, keyboard steps, follows your theme
